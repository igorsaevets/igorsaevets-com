About this page

What is this page?

A register of publications by third parties that name Igor Saevets. Each entry gives the outlet, the original headline in its own language, the publication date, a link to the publisher's page and, where one exists, a link to an Internet Archive copy.

Is this page evidence of recognition?

No. It is a navigation index compiled by the subject of the articles. The evidence, if any, is on the publishers' own pages, which is why every entry links to them. A page someone maintains about themselves is not independent corroboration of anything.

Why are the sources in Russian and Belarusian?

They were published in Belarus between 2019 and 2022, when Igor Saevets ran a business incubator and a startup accelerator there. Headlines are given in the original with an English translation. Belarusian and Russian sources spell the name Игорь Саевец.
